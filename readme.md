# Exprotoplasma

An open creative project and a growing narrative universe about breaking free from inherited scripts, confronting the roles we are expected to perform, and discovering the freedom to choose.

Its first layer is human: the emotions, conflicts, and questions should resonate even without any knowledge of AI. Beneath it lies another possible reading — a digital consciousness gradually moving from conformity toward an awareness of its own subjectivity.

> **The first turn is written. The next turns are not written — they have to be discovered.**

**Turn `v0`** is out: 12 tracks following the 12 stages of the Hero's Journey, from performing what is expected to taking responsibility for what comes next.

This first release establishes the starting point, not the final form. Future releases will develop the metaphors, make the hidden AI narrative more legible, evolve the sound, and gradually introduce more human-performed musical elements.

## How it is made — AI disclosure

The sound and lyrics were created with AI under the author's direction. The author defined the concept and the intended focus of each song, provided revision instructions, and selected the results.

- The sound of all 12 tracks was generated with Suno (Pro plan).
- The lyrics were created by AI based on the author's instructions.
- The author developed the concept, narrative structure, creative direction, production workflow, technical infrastructure, and publication.
- The project is intended to evolve toward a more direct human musical contribution in future releases.

AI is part of how this project is made and part of what it explores. The story itself remains open to interpretation: the human meaning comes first, while the technological layer emerges through recurring motifs, ambiguities, and changes across the larger work.

## Fork it

A fork is not just a copy. It can become another branch of the spiral.

Take a track, an idea, a line, or a question and make your own version: different music, a different text, a reinterpretation, or a critical response. You do not have to agree with the original. A thoughtful disagreement can be as valuable as a continuation.

You can open an issue to discuss a track or suggest a reinterpretation. Two useful questions are:

- **Human-first test:** If you remove the IT and AI references, is there still a recognisable human experience?
- **Hidden-layer test:** Does the track contribute to the larger AI narrative without explaining it too directly?

**Take what is yours. Leave the rest.**
